Part 1 & 2: Core Philosophy & Construction

Understanding how scale degrees map directly into playable chord structures.

1

Major & Major 7th

Formula: $1 - 3 - 5 - 7$

The foundation of major harmony. The Major 3rd provides a bright quality, while the Major 7th adds a soft, lush resolution.

Example: $Cmaj7$
Notes: $C - E - G - B$
Drop-2 Shape: $A$-string root drop-2 voicing.
2

Minor 7th

Formula: $1 - \flat3 - 5 - \flat7$

Flatted 3rd ($\flat3$) creates a somber quality, and the flatted 7th ($\flat7$) relaxes the tension for blues, jazz, and pop.

Example: $Dm7$
Notes: $D - F - A - C$
Barre Shape: Low $E$-string root standard barre.
3

Dominant 7th

Formula: $1 - 3 - 5 - \flat7$

Combines a bright Major 3rd with a bluesy Minor 7th ($\flat7$). Creates strong harmonic tension pulling toward the home chord.

Example: $G7$
Notes: $G - B - D - F$
Open Shape: Standard cowboy chord shape.
